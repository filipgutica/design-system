Color

Ratios are WCAG contrast against each named background: 4.5:1 for text, and 3:1 for icons, marks, and control edges. A ratio below its target says so in text.

Shape

Pill buttons, rounded fields and toggle cards, large-radius panels, and inline corners. Cards never take a pill.

  • --radius-inlinePlaces and picked rows: navigation rows, tabs, list rows, breadcrumb links, checkboxes, and keyboard keys
  • --radius-fieldTyped fields, controls whose label wraps, and one-line controls that are not actions: inputs, selects, textareas, toggle cards, pager links, the project-menu trigger, the labelled contents trigger and its no-JavaScript summary, and the skip link
  • --radius-controlPills that act, or set a value in place with every option shown: buttons other than the labelled contents trigger, quiet actions other than the project-menu trigger, segmented controls, and their thumbs
  • --radius-panelContainers: wells, plates, panels, radio cards, alerts, and floating layers
  • --radius-pillSmall state marks: status badges, switch tracks, slider and progress tracks, and score pips

Focus and motion

  • --focus-width solid --color-focusOne ring for every role but fields: buttons, quiet actions, links, tabs, and navigation rows. --color-focus is the accent, at 3:1 or more on every surface. It has no glow and sits over the control's own raise or sink shadow. Pointer hover never draws focus.
  • --focus-offsetThe gap between a control and its ring
  • 1px --color-accent edge, 2px inset lineFields draw no ring. Focus turns the edge --color-accent with a 2px line of the accent at 18% inside it; the fill stays. An invalid field keeps its --color-error edge and line. In forced colors a Highlight outline replaces the line.
  • offset -(--focus-width)Only inside a scrolling or clipping parent, or a segmented control, with a comment that names the reason
  • --duration-fast
    Hover and press
  • --duration
    Menus, panels, and the switch knob
  • --ease-out
    Animate opacity or transform only where it explains a state change
  • prefers-reduced-motion
    The tokens.css base sets transitions and animations to 0ms and turns off smooth scrolling. A busy spinner slows to 1.4s instead.
